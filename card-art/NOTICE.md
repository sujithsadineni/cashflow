# Card art

These pictures show what particular credit cards look like, so the app can offer one when a
statement for that card is imported (Settings → Accounts or Cards → Customize this card; it's
never applied without being picked, and can be removed any time).

They are the card issuers' own published artwork. The names, logos and designs on them are
trademarks and copyrighted works of their respective owners (American Express, Apple and
Goldman Sachs, Bank of America, Bilt and Wells Fargo, Barclays and Frontier Airlines,
Robinhood, Zolve, Mastercard, Visa). They are included only to identify the card a statement
belongs to; this project isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. The names printed on
some pictures are the issuers' own sample names, not real cardholders.

If you own one of these images and want it removed, open an issue and it will be taken out.
